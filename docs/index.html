Solar asset assessment

Every utility-scale solar plant in India above 50 MW in the WRI Global Power Plant Database, tested against what satellites actually observed at the site. Five checks, published thresholds, one card per asset.

This exists to answer a narrow question: can a reported asset record be independently corroborated from free data, quickly enough to be worth doing before a diligence site visit? The answer is partly, and the cards are explicit about which part.

How the score is built

CheckWeightFires when
Build timing30 observed build more than 12 months after the reported commissioning year ends
Resource shortfall20 mean annual GHI anomaly below -3.0% against 1991-2020
Thermal derating15 more than 60 days a year above 40°C
Vegetation15 NDVI trend inside the footprint above +0.010 per year
Flood exposure20 surface-water occurrence above 10% or height above drainage under 5 m

What this does not do

  • The footprint is a capacity-scaled circle, not a segmented panel array. It over-includes surrounding land, which dilutes every area statistic.
  • Height above nearest drainage is approximated from a DEM neighbourhood minimum. It is a screening proxy, not a hydraulic flood model.
  • A flag is a reason to look, never a finding. Nothing here is evidence of misreporting by any operator.
  • Unresolved checks score as clear, so a score is a lower bound. Each card states how much of the rubric was resolved.
  • Earth Engine's free tier is for non-commercial use. This is a personal methodology prototype.
