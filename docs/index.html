What it will show

Five checks against each reported asset, with published thresholds:

CheckWeightEvidence
Build timing30Dynamic World built + bare share of footprint, monthly since 2017
Resource shortfall20ERA5-Land annual GHI against the 1991–2020 mean
Thermal derating15ERA5-Land daily maximum temperature, NOCT cell model
Vegetation15Sentinel-2 annual median NDVI inside the footprint
Flood exposure20JRC Global Surface Water and Copernicus DEM terrain

What this does not do

  • The footprint is a capacity-scaled circle, not a segmented panel array.
  • Height above nearest drainage is a DEM screening proxy, not a hydraulic model.
  • A flag is a reason to look, never a finding. Nothing here is evidence of misreporting by any operator.
  • Earth Engine's free tier is for non-commercial use. This is a personal methodology prototype.

Data: Sentinel-2 (ESA/Copernicus), Dynamic World (Google/WRI), ERA5-Land (ECMWF/Copernicus), JRC Global Surface Water, Copernicus DEM GLO-30, WRI Global Power Plant Database. Code MIT licensed.
